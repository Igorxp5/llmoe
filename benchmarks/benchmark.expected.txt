
The number of planets in the Solar System has changed over time as our understanding of the Solar System and our galaxy has evolved. As of now, the official number of planets in the Solar System is eight:

1. Earth
2. Mars
3. Jupiter
4. Saturn
5. Uranus
6. Neptune
7. Pluto
8. Ceres (a dwarf planet)

However, this list may change in the future as new discoveries are made and our understanding of the Solar System and its structure evolves. In 2006, the International Astronomical Union (IAU) redefined the term "planet" and established a new category called "dwarf planets" for objects that orbit the Sun and are not large enough to clear their orbit of debris. Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet, and Ceres was promoted to the status of dwarf planet.

It's worth noting that the number of planets in the Solar System may continue to change as new discoveries are made and our understanding of the Solar System and its structure evolves.
